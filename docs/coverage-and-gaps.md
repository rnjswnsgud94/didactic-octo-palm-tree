# Coverage and gaps

Catalog version: `2026.08.20-mvp.1`  
Legal public-page review: `2026-08-20`  
Snapshot mode: `SNAPSHOT_ONLY`

## Covered common layer

- industrial-complex occupancy contract and its separate factory-approval deeming relation;
- off-site factory-establishment approval at the 500㎡ boundary and optional under-500㎡ coordination route;
- basic building permit/check, construction-start report and use-approval route;
- industrial-complex/off-site factory completion-report branch;
- conditionally displayed air/water/PSM review based on externally confirmed facts;
- practical early utility consultation with no invented duration.

## Geographic and industry scope

- Nationwide common rules are active.
- Cheongju and Cheonan scenarios are supplied, but local ordinances, delegated duties and detailed civil-service standards are not yet collected.
- Generic manufacturing, semiconductor/electronics and secondary-battery/chemical scenarios are covered only to choose questions and examples. Industry labels do not prove environmental or safety coverage.

## Material gaps

1. Cheongju/Cheonan ordinances, delegated-authority tables and local processing guidance.
2. Named industrial-complex management/development plans and allowable-industry codes.
3. Environmental and small-scale environmental impact-assessment thresholds.
4. Chemical-business permits, facility inspections, CAS/substance quantities.
5. Hazardous materials, high-pressure gas, fire and electrical pre-use inspections.
6. Capital-region control, farmland, forest, heritage, military and other site restrictions.
7. Exact building permit/report classification and local architectural ordinance.
8. Provider-specific power, water and wastewater capacity, contracts and lead times.
9. New industrial-complex designation, urban-management-plan change and major grid construction.

## Version warnings

- Air Act `2027-01-08` and Water Environment Act `2027-02-20` scheduled texts were visible during research. The current-effective versions and delegated facility tables remain a legal-review TODO.
